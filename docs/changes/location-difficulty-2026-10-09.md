# Location identity and novice difficulty repair

The opening called a forest an inn courtyard, patterned ground contained giant
false obstacles, repeated visits lacked identity, and later novice strategies
hit a difficulty cliff. This pass makes those problems concrete and verifiable.

- Sixteen dimensional location landmarks plus four pure ground surfaces and a
  pure water texture establish inns, piers, markets, clinics, shrines and cities.
- Courtyards, canal crossings and ritual partitions give different approaches
  on return visits; all15 biomes and repeated finale layouts remain distinct.
- Structure footprints block movement; Bridge cells carry movement over visible
  water. Open arch centres, connected pier water, contact shadows and clear
  boundary rims align the image with collision. Markers avoid roof overhangs.
- Buildings reveal when a footprint edge becomes known. Roof depth and temporary
  transparency keep players visible behind them; tall-window cameras and HUD
  adapt without shrinking text or losing the hero.
- Late boss attack caps at1.20 while HP stays; skill practice grows
  +2/+4/+8/+12 across chapters4–7. Actual story rewards are included in a separate
  novice experiment, without changing policies or granting unlimited resources.
- Enemy damage remains in the combat report after allied support. Guard/camp
  messages show actual capped restoration; visible首领 labels stay Chinese.

Validation:251 library tests,check/build;192 journeys with zero timeouts;
41-stage/7-seal/both-side-chain full audit;15 location stills, landscape/portrait,
450 raw frames and15-second video. Actual keyboard/pointer native review covers
opening, collision, roof transparency and a representative final battle.
