# UI and battle presentation polish

The old presentation mixed pixel-style text, stretched terrain art and crowded
command/status lists. Battle now has dedicated enemy health/intent, party vitals,
action descriptions and six icon cards; spell pages and loot support mouse and
keyboard input. Noto CJK and the ink/gold surface language are shared by all screens.

Eight painted perspective arenas replace stretched map textures. The hero uses
24 coherent painted animation frames, with real walking and sword poses. Contact
shadows, anticipation/recovery/recoil and softly shaded spell rings/shards improve
how characters and effects sit in the scene. Inventory uses the same hero artwork.

Map HUD has one objective tracker. Dialogue/inventory hide the map HUD; dialogue
portraits keep their square aspect and absent portraits reserve no blank column.
Inventory starts with useful character/build information, with complete journey
receipts at the end. Inventory/ending content remains available through wheel or
page-key scrolling.
